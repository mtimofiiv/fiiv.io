Originally started by Olivia Firth of Eco-Age, the __#30wearschallenge__ is a social challenge to wear garments 30 times or more.

I built a mobile app to facilitate this for a eco hackathon. You could add your garments, and track how many times you wear them.

It really took off! A lot of people used it - __about 2 thousand active users__, in fact. But unfortunately, I just don't have the time to maintain it, so I had to take the sad step to discontinue it.

Thank you to all our users!

## Technologies used

- Node.js/Javascript backend server with a PostgreSQL database
- Expo/React Native application built to Android and iOS
- Deploys with Docker
